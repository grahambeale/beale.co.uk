About

Background

  • Yell NPS: -1 → +47
  • Mimecast team: 4 → 8 across 3 disciplines
  • 25+ years, 8 organisations
  • ISO 30071-1:2019 accessibility programme delivered

I make complex systems work for the people who use them. Twenty-five years across media, finance, telecoms, edtech and cybersecurity, and the thread has stayed the same: find what an organisation isn't ready to tackle, build the evidence to make the case, then create the team and product conditions that make the work stick.

At Yell, consumer NPS was sitting at -1 and Google was eating into the core business model. I led the research and validation behind a two-sided marketplace concept, tested with twelve vertical competitors and twelve users across two groups. It launched and reached a touchpoint NPS of 47.

At Mimecast, I built three practices from nothing: a research function aligned to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, an accessibility programme built to ISO 30071-1:2019, and Mimecast Elements, a design system that became the shared standard across the product portfolio. When the organisation moved to an AI-first operating model, I turned the design function into a guild, with designers prototyping in Figma Make and engineering leaders running Cursor training for the team.

That experience shaped how I think about AI in product organisations now. When AI removes the technical barriers between roles, the question stops being about capability and starts being about legitimacy: who has the right to do what, and who's accountable for the result. Legitimacy comes from outcome ownership, not from who held the skill first. I call the conditions that make that possible care capital: the confidence, collaboration and open critique that let people exercise genuine judgement instead of just executing.

I'm looking for a director or VP-level design leadership role where design, product and engineering work as genuine partners, and where human-centred AI is central to the work rather than a line in the job spec.
